# Project brief (intake note) — Minimalistic modern family home, Kerala

> **Status:** initial intake note. **Superseded by the formal brief in
> [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md)**, which is the authoritative requirements document and carries the
> acceptance criteria. This note is kept for the record of the original ask.

## The ask

Build a **minimalistic modern house** for the owner and a **4-member family** (5 people total)
on a **5-acre plot** in a **village in Kerala**. The design should **show the beauty of the
surrounding nature** as much as the building itself.

## Budget

- **Target:** 100k (currency to be confirmed — see [`budget.md`](budget.md)).
- The budget is the primary constraint. Scope, materials and area are sized to fit it.

## Site

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Location | Village, Kerala, India |
| Plot size | 5 acres |
| Context | Rural / natural setting |
| Climate | Tropical monsoon — hot, humid, heavy seasonal rain |

## Design intent

- **Minimalistic, modern** — clean lines, uncluttered, honest materials.
- **Nature-forward** — large openings, framed views, indoor–outdoor flow, a small footprint on
  a large plot so the landscape leads.
- **Climate-appropriate** — deep overhangs / verandahs for sun and monsoon, cross-ventilation,
  a pitched or sloped roof to shed heavy rain.

## Occupants

- 5 people (owner + 4 family members).
- Bedroom count, accessibility needs and lifestyle requirements: **to be confirmed.**
