# Design brief & requirements — Minimalistic modern family home, Kerala

> **Status:** PROPOSED to the commander. This is the formal, authoritative design brief for
> the project. It supersedes the intake note in [`brief.md`](brief.md) and sets the
> requirements and acceptance criteria the design must satisfy before build begins.
> **Nothing is built until the commander approves this brief.**

## 1. Summary

Design and build a **minimalistic modern house** for a **family of five** (owner + 4 members)
on a **5-acre plot** in a **village in Kerala, India**, for a **budget cap of USD 100,000**.
The house is small against a large, green plot: the architecture frames and celebrates the
surrounding nature as much as it shelters the family.

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Occupants | 5 (owner + 4 family members) |
| Site | 5 acres, village, Kerala |
| Style | Minimalistic, modern |
| Budget cap | **USD 100,000** (hard ceiling, all-in build cost) |
| Defining intent | Frame and celebrate the surrounding nature |

## 2. Site & climate

| Attribute | Value | Design consequence |
|-----------|-------|--------------------|
| Location | Village, Kerala, India | Rural, low-density; privacy is easy, services may be limited |
| Plot size | 5 acres (~2 ha) | Small footprint, generous setbacks, landscape leads |
| Context | Natural / rural setting — likely greenery, possible slope | Orient to the best views and prevailing breeze |
| Climate | Tropical monsoon — hot, humid, heavy seasonal rain (Jun–Sep) | Rain, sun and humidity are the primary design drivers |
| Temperature | Warm year-round (roughly 23–35 °C) | Passive cooling over mechanical air-conditioning |

**Climate implications the design must answer:**

- **Monsoon:** sloped/pitched roof with deep overhangs, raised plinth above flood/splash line,
  covered circulation, well-drained site, rainwater harvesting.
- **Humidity & heat:** cross-ventilation through every occupied room, shaded openings, thermal
  mass and/or light-coloured roof, no sealed glass boxes that trap heat.
- **Sun:** verandahs and overhangs shade walls and glazing; orient large openings away from
  harsh west sun where views allow.

## 3. Design intent

- **Minimalistic, modern** — clean lines, uncluttered volumes, honest materials, restrained
  palette. Detail is quiet; the landscape is the ornament.
- **Nature-forward** — a **small footprint on a large plot** so the land dominates. Large
  openings, framed views, and strong indoor–outdoor flow (verandahs, courtyards, sit-outs).
  Rooms open to green, not to walls.
- **Natural light** — daylight in every habitable room; deep openings and, where useful, a
  courtyard or clerestory to pull light and air into the plan's centre.
- **Cross-ventilation** — openings on opposite/adjacent walls in every room so Kerala's breeze
  does the cooling. The plan is one-room-deep where it can be.
- **Locally sourced, climate-tested materials** — laterite, exposed brick, local timber,
  filler-slab or sloped tiled roof, oxide/kota/red-cement floors. Local materials cut cost,
  suit the climate, and root the house in its place.

## 4. Accommodation (programme)

Sized for a family of 5 within the budget cap. Final areas are set by the design team but must
cover this programme.

| Space | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Bedrooms | **3** — 1 primary (en-suite), 2 further bedrooms for the family of five |
| Bathrooms | 2–3 (1 en-suite + shared) |
| Living / dining | Open, connected living–dining that opens to a verandah/outdoor space |
| Kitchen | Functional kitchen with cross-ventilation and, ideally, a utility/work area |
| Verandah / sit-out | Covered outdoor living — the transition between house and land |
| Circulation | Minimal, naturally lit and ventilated |
| Storey | Single storey preferred (accessibility, cost, low profile in the landscape); a compact second storey is acceptable only if it keeps the footprint and budget down |
| Optional (confirm) | Pooja space, home office/study, guest provision, car porch |

**Indicative built area:** roughly **1,500–2,200 sq ft** finished — the range the USD 100,000
cap supports at Kerala rates (see [`budget.md`](budget.md)). Exact area is a design output, not
an input, and must be reconciled against the budget.

## 5. Constraints & assumptions

- **Budget cap USD 100,000 is a hard ceiling** for the all-in build cost. Scope, area, and
  material grade are sized to fit it, not the other way round.
- **Currency confirmed as USD** for this brief (was flagged as open in the intake note; the
  ticket sets the cap at USD 100,000).
- Local body approvals, survey and permits are in scope and costed (see `budget.md`).
- Kerala construction rate assumed ~₹2,000–3,500 / sq ft for a modern finish — **to be
  verified with 2–3 local contractors** before costs are fixed.

## 6. Acceptance criteria — what the design must meet

The design is accepted only when **all** of the following are demonstrably true:

- [ ] **Within budget** — total all-in build cost is **≤ USD 100,000**, evidenced by a costed
      line-item plan reconciled to the design's actual area (not the placeholder in `budget.md`).
- [ ] **Sleeps and serves 5** — at least **3 bedrooms**, adequate bathrooms, and open
      living–dining–kitchen sized for a family of five.
- [ ] **Monsoon-ready** — sloped/pitched roof with deep overhangs, raised plinth, and site
      drainage / rainwater harvesting suited to Kerala's heavy seasonal rain.
- [ ] **Humidity- & heat-appropriate** — every habitable room has **cross-ventilation** and
      shaded openings; the design relies on passive cooling, not sealed air-conditioning.
- [ ] **Daylight everywhere** — every habitable room is naturally lit; no dark internal rooms.
- [ ] **Open to nature** — large framed openings and real indoor–outdoor flow (verandah /
      courtyard / sit-out); a small footprint that lets the 5-acre landscape lead.
- [ ] **Local materials** — the material palette is predominantly locally sourced and
      climate-tested (laterite / brick / local timber / tiled or filler-slab roof).
- [ ] **Minimalistic & modern** — clean lines, restrained palette, honest materials; the
      design reads as modern and uncluttered.

## 7. Deliverables that follow this brief

1. Site plan and orientation study (sun, breeze, views, drainage).
2. Floor plan(s) and elevations under `designs/`.
3. Costed budget reconciled to the design (replaces the placeholder in `budget.md`).
4. Material schedule (local-first).

## 8. Open items for the commander

These do not block approval of the brief but refine the design:

1. **Storeys:** single confirmed as preferred — accept, or require two?
2. **Must-have spaces:** pooja room, home office/study, guest provision, car porch — which are in?
3. **Material preference:** any strong preference among laterite, exposed brick, or exposed concrete?
4. **Site specifics:** slope, existing trees/water, access road, and the direction of the best views.
