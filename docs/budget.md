The two readings of "100k" give very different projects:

| Reading | Rough buildable area at Kerala rates* | Implication |
|---------|----------------------------------------|-------------|
| **₹100,000 (1 lakh)** | Not viable for a full house | Would cover only design/permits or a small structure |
| **$100,000 (≈ ₹83 lakh)** | ~1,500–2,200 sq ft finished home | A comfortable minimalistic modern home is achievable |

\* Indicative only — Kerala construction runs roughly ₹2,000–3,500 / sq ft for a modern
finish (to be verified with local contractors). **Assume $100,000 until told otherwise.**
